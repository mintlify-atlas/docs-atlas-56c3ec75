Horion's Player category groups together modules that improve your character management and quality of life without being purely combat- or movement-oriented. This includes automated inventory management, armor equipping, totem handling, eating speed, interaction tweaks, and anti-detection helpers. Most of these modules operate quietly in the background each game tick.

---

## AutoArmor

Automatically equips the best available armor from your inventory into the appropriate armor slots each tick.

**Tooltip:** *"AutoArmor"* · No configurable settings. Requires your crafting/inventory screen to be accessible.

---

## AutoGapple

Automatically consumes a golden apple from your inventory when your health drops below a threshold.

**Tooltip:** *"AutoGapple"* · No configurable settings. Monitors health each tick and triggers eating via the move input handler.

---

## AutoSneak

Keeps your character in a permanent sneak state without requiring you to hold the sneak key.

**Tooltip:** *"AutoSneak"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `doSilent` | bool | false | Apply sneak silently (server does not see sneak state) |

---

## AutoTotem

Automatically moves a totem of undying from your inventory into your offhand slot, ensuring you always have one ready.

**Tooltip:** *"AutoTotem"* · No configurable settings. Checks and corrects your offhand each tick.

---

## AutoSprint *(Player)*

Keeps you sprinting continuously without needing to hold the sprint key. Identical functionality to the Movement category variant.

**Tooltip:** *"AutoSprint"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `alldirections` | bool | true | Sprint in all directions, not just forward |

<Note>
  AutoSprint appears in the **Movement** category in the source (`Category::MOVEMENT`). It is documented here for completeness since it benefits player utility as well.
</Note>

---

## InventoryCleaner

Scans your inventory and automatically discards items it considers useless based on your configured keep-list preferences.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically throws not needed stuff out of your inventory"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Tools` | bool | true | Keep tools (pickaxes, shovels, axes, etc.) |
| `Armor` | bool | true | Keep armor pieces |
| `Food` | bool | true | Keep food items |
| `Blocks` | bool | true | Keep placeable blocks |
| `OpenInv` | bool | true | Open your inventory screen if needed to perform the clean |
| `AutoSort` | bool | false | Automatically sort inventory after cleaning |

---

## InventoryMove

Allows you to move your character using WASD while the inventory screen is open.

**Tooltip:** *"InventoryMove"* · No configurable settings.

<Note>
  InventoryMove is categorized under **Movement** in the source (`Category::MOVEMENT`).
</Note>

---

## FastEat

Speeds up the food eating animation and consumption rate so you restore hunger more quickly.

**Tooltip:** *"FastEat"* · No configurable settings. Applies a tick override while eating.

---

## NoSlowDown *(Player)*

Prevents the movement speed penalty applied when holding items such as bows, food, and shields.

**Tooltip:** *"NoSlowDown"* · No configurable settings. Operates via memory patch (opcode override).

<Note>
  NoSlowDown is categorized under **Movement** in the source (`Category::MOVEMENT`).
</Note>

---

## NoSwing

Suppresses the arm-swing animation when you attack or use an item, hiding the visual tell from spectators.

**Tooltip:** *"NoSwing"* · No configurable settings.

---

## NoHurtcam

Disables the screen-shake effect that plays whenever your character takes damage.

**Tooltip:** *"NoHurtcam"* · No configurable settings.

<Note>
  NoHurtcam is categorized under **Visual** in the source (`Category::VISUAL`).
</Note>

---

## StackableItem

Forces normally unstackable items (such as tools, weapons, and armor) to stack in your inventory.

**Tooltip:** *"StackableItem"* · No configurable settings. Applied each tick while enabled.

---

## MidClick

Binds an action to your middle mouse button, triggering interactions with entities or blocks on middle-click.

**Tooltip:** *"MidClick"* · No configurable settings. Detects middle mouse presses each tick.

---

## Reach

Extends your melee attack and block interaction distance beyond the default vanilla range.

**Tooltip:** *"Increases your reach"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Reach Value` | float | 3.0 | 3.0 – 7.0 | Custom reach distance in blocks |

<Note>
  Reach is categorized under **Combat** in the source (`Category::COMBAT`).
</Note>

---

## Hitbox

Artificially enlarges the hitboxes of nearby entities, making them easier to click on and attack.

**Tooltip:** *"Increase an entity's hitbox size"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Height` | float | 2.0 | 1.8 – 10 | Expanded hitbox height in blocks |
| `Width` | float | 4.0 | 0.6 – 10 | Expanded hitbox width in blocks |

<Note>
  Hitbox is categorized under **Combat** in the source (`Category::COMBAT`).
</Note>

---

## EditionFaker

Spoofs the Minecraft edition reported to the server, allowing a Bedrock client to appear as a different edition to the server.

**Tooltip:** *"EditionFaker"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| *(no registered settings)* | — | — | Edition is determined internally via `getFakedEdition()` |

<Note>
  EditionFaker is categorized under **Misc** in the source (`Category::MISC`).
</Note>

---

## AntiImmobile

Prevents the server from flagging your client as immobile or "frozen," keeping movement packets flowing normally.

**Tooltip:** *"AntiImmobile"* · No configurable settings.

<Note>
  AntiImmobile is categorized under **Misc** in the source (`Category::MISC`).
</Note>

---

## AntiBot

Filters out bot entities from being targeted by combat modules, using multiple detection heuristics.

**Tooltip:** *"Enable this module to filter bots!"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Hitbox Check` | bool | true | Ignore entities with zero-size hitboxes |
| `Invisible Check` | bool | true | Ignore invisible entities |
| `EntityID Check` | bool | true | Ignore entities with unusual ID patterns |
| `Other Check` | bool | true | Apply miscellaneous heuristic checks |
| `Extra Check` | bool | false | Enable experimental additional detection logic |

---

## ChestAura

Automatically opens nearby chest containers by sending interaction packets without requiring you to click on them manually.

**Tooltip:** *"Aura for opening chests"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Range` | int | 3 | 1 – 10 | Radius in blocks to scan for chests |
| `EnderChests` | bool | false | — | Also interact with ender chests |

---

## ChestStealer

When a chest is open, automatically transfers all items from the chest into your inventory.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically takes all items out of a chest"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Close Delay` | int | 7 | 0 – 20 | Ticks to wait after stealing before closing the chest |
| `enhanced` | bool | true | — | Use enhanced packet-level transfer instead of simulated clicks |

---

## ExtendedBlockReach

Increases the maximum distance at which you can place and break blocks.

**Tooltip:** *"Extended reach for placing/breaking blocks"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `reach` | float | 7.0 | 7.0 – 20.0 | Maximum block interaction range in blocks |

---

## Nbt

Reads NBT (Named Binary Tag) data from blocks you look at and copies it to your clipboard.

**Tooltip:** *"Nbt"* · No configurable settings. Runs each tick and copies new NBT data when the pointed-at block changes.

<Note>
  Nbt is categorized under **Player** in the source (`Category::PLAYER`).
</Note>
