Horion's Player category groups together modules that improve your character management and quality of life without being purely combat- or movement-oriented. This includes automated inventory management, armor equipping, totem handling, eating speed, interaction tweaks, and anti-detection helpers. Most of these modules operate quietly in the background each game tick.

---

## AutoArmor

Automatically equips the best available armor from your inventory into the appropriate armor slots each tick.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically equips the best armor"* · No configurable settings. Requires your crafting/inventory screen to be accessible.

---

## AutoTotem

Automatically moves a totem of undying from your inventory into your offhand slot, ensuring you always have one ready.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically puts totems into your offhand"* · No configurable settings. Checks and corrects your offhand each tick.

---

## AntiBot

Filters out bot entities so that combat modules do not waste attacks on them.

**Tooltip:** *"Enable this module to filter bots!"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Hitbox Check` | bool | true | Ignore entities with zero-size hitboxes |
| `Invisible Check` | bool | true | Ignore invisible entities |
| `EntityID Check` | bool | true | Ignore entities with unusual ID patterns |
| `Other Check` | bool | true | Apply additional heuristic bot checks |
| `Extra Check` | bool | false | Enable experimental extra bot detection |

---

## InventoryCleaner

Scans your inventory and automatically discards items it considers useless based on your configured keep-list preferences.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically throws not needed stuff out of your inventory"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Tools` | bool | true | Keep tools (pickaxes, shovels, axes, etc.) |
| `Armor` | bool | true | Keep armor pieces |
| `Food` | bool | true | Keep food items |
| `Blocks` | bool | true | Keep placeable blocks |
| `OpenInv` | bool | true | Open your inventory screen if needed to perform the clean |
| `AutoSort` | bool | false | Automatically sort inventory after cleaning |

---

## FastEat

Speeds up the food eating animation and consumption rate so you restore hunger more quickly.

**Tooltip:** *"Eat food almost instant"* · No configurable settings. Applies a tick override while eating.

---

## NoSwing

Suppresses the arm-swing animation when you attack or use an item, hiding the visual tell from spectators.

**Tooltip:** *"Disable arm swing animation"* · No configurable settings.

---

## StackableItem

Forces normally unstackable items (such as tools, weapons, and armor) to stack in your inventory.

**Tooltip:** *"Stack items you normally wouldn't be able to stack (e. g. swords)"* · No configurable settings. Applied each tick while enabled.

---

## MidClick

Binds an action to your middle mouse button — clicking a player with your scroll wheel adds them as a friend.

**Tooltip:** *"Click a player with your mouse wheel to add as friend"* · No configurable settings.

---

## NoFriends

Ignores the friend list check in targeting modules, allowing them to attack players on your friends list.

**Tooltip:** *"Ignores friend list check"* · No configurable settings.

---

## ChestAura

Automatically opens nearby chest containers by sending interaction packets without requiring you to click on them manually.

**Tooltip:** *"Aura for opening chests"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Range` | int | 3 | 1 – 10 | Radius in blocks to scan for chests |
| `EnderChests` | bool | false | — | Also interact with ender chests |

---

## ChestStealer

When a chest is open, automatically transfers all items from the chest into your inventory.

**Tooltip:** *"Automatically takes all items out of a chest"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Close Delay` | int | 7 | 0 – 20 | Ticks to wait after stealing before closing the chest |
| `enhanced` | bool | true | — | Use enhanced packet-level transfer instead of simulated clicks |

---

## ExtendedBlockReach

Increases the maximum distance at which you can place and break blocks.

**Tooltip:** *"Exteneded reach for placing/breaking blocks"*

| Setting | Type | Default | Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `reach` | float | 7.0 | 7.0 – 20.0 | Maximum block interaction range in blocks |

---

## Nbt

Reads NBT (Named Binary Tag) data from mobs you right-click and prints it. Only works on local worlds.

**Tooltip:** *"Print NBT tags, right click on mobs (Only works on local worlds!)"* · No configurable settings.
